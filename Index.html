BIO 2 Laboratory & Curriculum Module

Instructional Guide: Intracellular Protein Sorting

This interactive module guides students through the molecular logic of eukaryotic secretory and endocytic trafficking—from signal peptide recognition at the Rough ER to SNARE-mediated membrane fusion at the plasma membrane.

01 Student Learning Objectives

  • • Co-translational Entry: Explain how SRP arrests translation and docks ribosomes onto the Sec61 translocon complex.
  • • Vesicle Budding & Coat Selection: Distinguish the roles of Sar1/COPII (anterograde), ARF1/COPI (retrograde), and Clathrin/AP1/GGA (TGN-to-endosome).
  • • Directional Cytoskeletal Transport: Contrast Dynein-driven minus-end transport (ER → Golgi) with Kinesin-driven plus-end transport (TGN → PM).
  • • Target Specificity & Fusion: Describe how Rab GTPases, tethering factors, and four-helix v-SNARE/t-SNARE bundles overcome membrane hydration barriers.

02 Guided 3D Exploration Protocol

  1. Switch to 3D Simulation and click the Rough ER to observe membrane-bound ribosomes and Sec61 channels.
  2. Activate Pathway 2 (Anterograde) and trace green COPII-coated vesicles budding from ERES and fusing into the ERGIC/Cis-Golgi.
  3. Activate Pathway 3 (Retrograde) to observe yellow COPI vesicles returning escaped ER-resident chaperones (BiP/PDI) via KDEL receptors.
  4. Activate Pathway 4 (Lysosomal) to follow red Clathrin-coated vesicles pinched off by Dynamin toward Late Endosomes.
  5. Activate Pathway 5 (Exocytosis) to inspect SNARE zippering at the Plasma Membrane.
